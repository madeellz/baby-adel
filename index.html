When you know:

When will we know?

Timing depends on the testing method and on what Marwa's doctor offers. As a general guide:

From around week 10

NIPT / cell-free DNA blood test

A blood test from the mother that screens for certain chromosome conditions. Where it's offered and requested, it can sometimes also report the baby's sex chromosomes — commonly from about 10 weeks.

Often weeks 18–22

Ultrasound / anatomy scan

Sex can sometimes be seen on ultrasound, most commonly around the detailed anatomy scan at about 18–22 weeks. It isn't always visible — it depends on the baby's position and other factors. Some clinics offer an earlier guess, but it's less reliable.
